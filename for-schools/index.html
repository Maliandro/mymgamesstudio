What Prof. Nano is

Prof. Nano is a hands-on science game for children aged 7 to 10. A friendly professor explains out loud while children experiment and see what happens on screen. Eight regions hold 48 short mini games, from atoms to stars and from the heart to robots.

  • Ages 7–10Short activities, read-aloud explanations
  • 48 mini gamesAcross 8 regions, all hands-on
  • Works offlineNo internet needed after the download
  • No adsNothing to tap by accident
  • No data collectionNo accounts, no tracking
  • Parent gatePurchases sit behind a parent gate

Free to try, one-time Full Version

The first region, Lighthouse Point, is free to play with no time limit, so a class, a family or a librarian can see exactly what children will do. The Full Version opens all 8 regions and every game. It is a single one-time purchase with no subscription, and it sits behind a parent gate.

The game runs in Turkish and English. Prof. Nano is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Android is coming soon.

For institutions and educators

Teachers, school librarians, public libraries and home-education groups are welcome to review Prof. Nano before deciding anything. Teachers, librarians and education reviewers can ask for a limited review code. We read each request; please write from your school or institution address and tell us where you teach or work. Review codes are for evaluating the game, not for classroom-wide use.

We do not offer bulk licences at this time. If that changes, this page will say so.
