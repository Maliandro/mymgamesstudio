The game

Foot Tennis Stars brings foot tennis to the phone: real foot tennis rules, a net, smash shots and rallies you can read. Players on iPhone and Android meet in the same online matches. The store page is available in 39 languages.

Federation

The game is made with respect for the sport. The name and emblem of the Turkish Foot Tennis Federation already appear inside the game. We would like to work with the federation and with clubs, leagues and events: a shared announcement, an official event or cup name inside the game, or a QR poster for a hall or a broadcast. We ask for nothing in return, and we only use a name or an emblem with written permission.

Stadium boards for sponsors

Every arena has advertising boards along the side and back of the pitch, the way a real match does. They are seen in every rally, every replay and in the Nations Cup final. A brand, a club or an event can have its own board in the game, together with a venue or cup name.

Partnership options are flexible: stadium boards, a named cup or a special in-game event. Get in touch and tell us what you have in mind; we will reply with a clear proposal that fits your goals. We do not publish prices on this page.

We do not count impressions inside the game. Any figure we give you about reach would be an estimate, and we will say so.
