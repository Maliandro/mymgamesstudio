Tomb Maze: Horror Escape is out today on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

If you read the earlier post, you know the idea: you are inside a tomb, the way out exists, and you are not alone in there. The game is built around a three-word line, *It hears you*, and that is all I will say about what is in there.

## What you get

- A first-person horror maze with 20 levels.
- Hiding places and traps to deal with while you look for the way out.
- A harder Nightmare mode and an Endless Descent mode once you finish.
- Six languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French.
- Free to play. Ads are optional to remove with a one-time purchase.

## How I'd play it

Headphones, a quiet room, lights low. Hurrying makes noise, and stopping has a cost, so the maze is a puzzle and a decision at the same time. Don't rush the first level; learn how it feels.

## A small request

This is a one-person project and a first release. If you play it, I would love to hear how far you got, what felt unfair, and what felt right. You can reach me through the support page in the app, or on the channels below. A short App Store review helps a small game more than you might think.

Thank you for playing.
