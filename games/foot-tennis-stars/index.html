Foot Tennis Stars

Foot Tennis Stars is a free foot tennis game for iPhone, iPad and Android. Football meets volleyball: two sides, a net, no hands, and rallies won with placed shots, lobs, smashes and bicycle kicks. Play against computer rivals without internet, or go online, where iPhone and Android players meet in the same match.

How it plays

Each side tries to send the ball over the net so the other side can't return it. The game follows the core rules of the real sport: you get up to three touches on your side, the ball may bounce once on your side, a second bounce loses the point, and if the ball lands out, the last player to touch it loses the point. The winner of a rally serves next, and a serve has to clear the net.

You run with your left thumb and play with buttons: PLACE for a controlled shot, LOB to go over a player at the net, SMASH to hit down hard, and BOUNCE to set the ball up for yourself. Hold SMASH for a bicycle kick; smash a high ball at the net for a scissor smash. In 2 v 2 there is a PASS button and teammates take turns, as in real doubles.

A match is one game to 7, 9 or 11 points (you choose; 9 is the default). Matches against the computer need a two-point lead, with a cap so a game can't go on forever. New to the sport? Read our guide How to Play Foot Tennis: Rules, Scoring and Tips.

Modes and features

  • Quick match against a computer rival, and Lightning match: first to 3 points, and at 2–2 the next point wins.
  • Online match: matchmaking by level. If nobody is found within about 20 seconds, you play a computer rival instead. Friends can join the same room with a six-digit code.
  • Play with a friend on the same Wi-Fi network, 1 v 1 or 2 v 2.
  • Season (a league of 10 teams), cups for 4, 8 or 16 players, and the Nations Cup with national teams, a tunnel walk-out and a trophy lift.
  • Daily challenge, training, and a ladder of rivals, each with their own style of play.
  • Your own player and club: create your character, design a kit and a crest, and build up a club ground.
  • Nine venues: stadium, beach, indoor hall, rooftop, park, snowy peak, desert, training ground and a small-sided pitch, plus the Nations Cup arena.

Who it's for

Players who like football, volleyball or quick sports games on the phone, people who play foot tennis and want it in their pocket, and friends who want to settle a rally on the same Wi-Fi. Matches are short, and you can play without an account.

Price and platforms

Foot Tennis Stars is free on the App Store (iPhone and iPad) and on Google Play. It shows ads between some matches and offers optional rewarded ads for extra gold. Optional purchases are a Club Membership, which removes the ads between matches and adds gold and extras, and gold packs. There is no subscription, no energy timer and no paid loot box. The store pages are available in 39 languages.

Frequently asked questions

Is Foot Tennis Stars free?

Yes. Foot Tennis Stars is free to download on the App Store and Google Play. It has ads between some matches and optional purchases: a Club Membership that removes those ads and adds gold, and gold packs. There is no subscription.

Can iPhone and Android players play together?

Yes. Online matches are cross-play: an iPhone player and an Android player can end up in the same match.

Can I play Foot Tennis Stars offline?

Yes. Matches against computer rivals work without an internet connection. Online matches need a connection.

What are the rules in Foot Tennis Stars?

Each side gets up to three touches and one bounce. A second bounce, or a ball that lands out, loses the point. The rally winner serves, and a serve must clear the net. A match is one game to 7, 9 or 11 points, and matches against the computer need a two-point lead.

Can I play foot tennis with a friend?

Yes. On the same Wi-Fi network you can play 1 v 1 or 2 v 2, and online a friend can join your room with a six-digit code.

Which languages does the game support?

The game itself is in English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Thai. The store pages are in 39 languages.
