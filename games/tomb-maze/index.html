Tomb Maze: Horror Escape

Tomb Maze: Horror Escape is a free first-person horror maze game for iPhone and iPad. The pyramid has collapsed behind you, and the only way out is through twenty dark labyrinths guarded by creatures that see and hear you. Hide, listen, and don't run.

How it plays

You are an explorer who came for the Sun Disk. Each level is a new maze, built fresh every time, and it gets larger, darker and more crowded as you go deeper. You have a flashlight, a pocket for spare batteries, a map and a compass, and you are looking for the way out.

  • Hide and listen. Duck into a locker or a sarcophagus and hold your breath. Running makes noise, and every footstep carries.
  • Three kinds of danger. The Guardian patrols the corridors; the Listener comes to sound; the Shadow freezes while you look at it, so never turn your back.
  • Traps and seals. Dart walls, spike pits, collapsing floors and falling stones. Sealed doors open when you read the glyphs and turn the dials.
  • Treasure. Pieces of the Sun Disk, hidden relics for your Treasure Room, and journal pages that tell what happened here.
  • Help when you're lost. Checkpoints, and Intuition hints when you are truly stuck.

Modes

  • Story: 20 levels.
  • Nightmare: unlocked when you finish the story. The same mazes with faster Guardians, no map and no checkpoints.
  • Endless Descent: one life, floor after floor. How deep can you go?
  • Daily Labyrinth: one maze for everyone each day, unlocked after level 3.

Game Center has three leaderboards (daily maze time, descent depth, fewest deaths in the story) and 15 achievements.

Who it's for

Players aged 12 and up who like short, tense horror escapes: one maze, one way out, and something in the dark between you and it. It is built for headphones; footsteps, breathing and the creatures' sounds tell you where the danger is.

Price and platforms

Tomb Maze is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and there is no paywall: all 20 levels are free. It shows ads between some levels and offers optional rewarded ads, for example to continue after you die. Optional purchases: Remove Ads, an Explorer Pack (no ads plus hints) and Intuition hint packs. There is no subscription. An Android version has not been released.

Frequently asked questions

Is Tomb Maze free?

Yes. Tomb Maze: Horror Escape is free on the App Store and all 20 levels are free. It has ads and optional purchases: Remove Ads, an Explorer Pack and Intuition hint packs.

How scary is Tomb Maze, and who is it for?

It is rated 12+ on the App Store. It has horror themes and jump scares but no gore. It is best played with headphones.

Does Tomb Maze work offline?

Yes. It works offline and needs no account or sign-in.

How many levels does Tomb Maze have?

The story has 20 levels. After it there is Nightmare mode, Endless Descent and a Daily Labyrinth that is the same for everyone each day.

Is Tomb Maze on Android?

Not yet. Tomb Maze is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

Which languages does Tomb Maze support?

English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, German and French.
