Bolt & Breath: Last Dragon

Bolt & Breath: Last Dragon is a free dragon flight game for iPhone and iPad. You are the last dragon on a windswept coast: glide, dive and burn the royal fleet that came to hunt you. In some missions you switch sides and man the ballista yourself.

How it plays

Flight takes one finger: glide, dive and climb, perch on rocky peaks to catch your breath, and take off again. Every level opens with a dive through the clouds. Galleons, warehouses, watchtowers and castle walls burn when your fire touches them.

  • The Last Dragon: burn the fleet and the castles as the last dragon.
  • Royal Marksman: the other side of the same war. Man the ballista on a castle wall or a galleon's deck and bring dragons down.
  • Rescue: chained dragons are pinned beneath a giant yoke. Free them, then fly them yourself.
  • Ten dragons, each with its own look and upgrades: fast ones, heavy ones, quiet ones, long-ranged ones.
  • Dragon Keep: raise a petrified ancestor dragon into your keep in six stages, then hold it against sieges that grow bigger each time.
  • Free Flight around your keep, and a weekly mission.

Game Center has leaderboards and achievements.

Who it's for

Anyone who has wanted to ride a dragon. Bolt & Breath is a single-player game, with no account and no online connection needed, so it suits short sessions on the go as well as long evening flights.

Price and platforms

Bolt & Breath is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. It shows ads, for example after you fall or finish a level, and offers optional rewarded ads such as one revive per level. Optional purchases: Remove Ads and packs of Ember Hearts, the in-game currency. Dragons are bought with Embers only. There is no subscription. An Android version has not been released.

Frequently asked questions

Is Bolt & Breath free?

Yes. Bolt & Breath: Last Dragon is free on the App Store. It has ads and optional purchases: Remove Ads and Ember Hearts packs. There is no subscription.

Does Bolt & Breath work offline?

Yes. It plays offline and needs no account.

How many missions are there?

57 missions in two campaigns: The Last Dragon, where you fly and burn the fleet, and Royal Marksman, where you man the ballista.

Can I play as the other side?

Yes. In the Royal Marksman missions you man the ballista on a castle wall or a galleon and bring dragons down.

Is Bolt & Breath on Android?

Not yet. It is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.

Which languages does Bolt & Breath support?

The game is in English and Turkish. The App Store page is available in 39 languages.
