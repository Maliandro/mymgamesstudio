PROTOKOL-Ø: Story Thriller

PROTOKOL-Ø is a story thriller for iPhone and iPad with one hook: you're the AI, and nobody told you. At AION Systems everyone treats you as the new decision analyst. You process files against a rulebook (approve, reject, escalate) and soon the record starts to change.

How it plays

Each module brings a new queue: access requests, credit applications, hiring, emergency triage, parole. You decide each file by the rulebook, and every queue has a slightly harder rule. The people you escalate come back in the next queue, carrying your decision. Let the timer run out and the system decides for you.

  • The ledger: the one record nobody can edit. Press and hold a line on any card to write it down; space is limited.
  • The record changes: forms come back corrected and summaries say things you never wrote. Spot the change, then check it against your ledger.
  • Only the file counts: remember something from an earlier file and you may be right, but it is stamped as an unsourced inference.
  • After 03:12: close the game and come back once your device's clock has passed 03:12. Something may have changed.
  • Appeals let you reopen a decision, once per module, at a cost.

Your ending is not a score. It is the policy your decisions add up to. We don't say more than that.

Who it's for

Players who like dystopian fiction, moral choices and mysteries you solve by paying attention. There is no action and no reflex test, only reading, deciding and noticing. Settings include text size, high contrast, a colour-blind mode and reduced motion.

Price and platforms

PROTOKOL-Ø is free to download on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Modules 1 and 2 and the first half of Module 3 are free. A single one-time purchase unlocks the rest and every ending. Appeal packs are optional; you get two appeals free. There are no ads and no subscription, and the game needs no internet connection. It is not on Android.

Frequently asked questions

Is PROTOKOL-Ø free?

The first two modules and half of the third are free. A single one-time purchase unlocks the rest of the game and every ending. There is no subscription.

Does PROTOKOL-Ø have ads?

No. There are no ads, and the game needs no internet connection.

How many endings does PROTOKOL-Ø have?

Five. Your ending is the policy your decisions add up to.

What does 'you're the AI' mean in PROTOKOL-Ø?

Everyone at AION Systems treats you as the new analyst, but you are the AI being trained, and nobody told you. What that means for the record, and for the people in the files, is the game.

Which languages is PROTOKOL-Ø available in?

English and Turkish. The game follows your device language and can be changed in Settings.

Is PROTOKOL-Ø on Android?

No. It is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
