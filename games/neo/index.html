NEO: Interactive Horror Story

NEO: Interactive Horror Story is a free text-based horror game for iPhone and iPad. Elif has been missing for three days and the campus clock froze at 17:34. You are Lika, the only one still looking for her. Read, choose and survive: the wrong door kills you, and dying restarts the loop.

How it plays

You read and you choose. Do you go to Elif's apartment, follow her trail on campus, or visit her family? There are no right answers, only consequences: force the wrong door and you die, make noise and you are seen. When you die, the loop starts again, and this time you know one more thing.

  • Choices that matter: hundreds of scenes, and you can never pick an option that refers to a clue you haven't found.
  • An ending map that fills in only as you reach each of the 20 endings.
  • A continue card shows your loop number and where you left off.
  • Hints point the way without giving you the answer.
  • Game Center achievements, and optional reminders at most twice a day.

That is all we will say about the story. NEO is built on not knowing, and the first time through is the only time you get to not know.

Who it's for

Readers who like psychological thrillers, mysteries and choose-your-own-adventure stories, and players who want horror built from tension and choices rather than gore. It is a game of reading, so it suits headphones-off moments too.

Price and platforms

NEO is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and the whole story, with all 20 endings, can be played for free. An ad may play after a death (never after your first one), and you can watch an optional ad for a hint. Full Access is an optional one-time purchase that removes the death ads, makes hints unlimited and opens Elif's Records. Signal packs let you rewind to the moment before a death. NEO is not on Android.

Frequently asked questions

Is NEO free?

Yes. NEO is free to download and the whole story, including all 20 endings, can be played for free. Full Access and Signal packs are optional purchases.

How many endings does NEO have?

NEO has 20 endings. They appear on the ending map only as you reach them.

What kind of game is NEO?

NEO is an interactive horror story: a text-based, choice-driven psychological thriller with a time loop. You read scenes and choose what to do next.

Which languages is NEO available in?

English, Turkish, German, French and Spanish. You can switch languages from the menu.

Is NEO on Android?

No. NEO is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
