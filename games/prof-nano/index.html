Prof. Nano: Kids Science Games

Prof. Nano is a hands-on science game for children aged 7 to 10, on iPhone and iPad. A friendly professor reads everything aloud while children experiment and see what happens: 48 mini games across 8 regions, from magnets and mirrors to the human body, space and robots. No ads, it works offline, and it collects no data.

How it plays

Children learn by doing, not by answering quizzes. Each mini game is a small experiment: move a magnet, tilt a ramp, build a circuit, and watch what happens. Prof. Nano explains out loud, and help comes in small steps when a child gets stuck.

  • 8 regions: physics, math, the human body, biology, space, history, chemistry and robotics.
  • Discovery cards to collect, and a discovery notebook of "I thought… I saw…" notes.
  • Try at home: 10 simple experiments to do together, away from the screen.
  • Everything is read aloud, so children who are still learning to read can play.

For parents

  • Parent gate: purchases and settings sit behind a gate that asks for a number written in words.
  • Parent section: what your child discovered, questions to ask them, and progress by region.
  • Break reminder every 15 minutes, on by default.
  • No ads, no data collection, no internet requests, and no links that lead children out of the game.

Price and platforms

Prof. Nano is free to download on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. The first region, Lighthouse Point, is free with no time limit and has 6 games. The Full Version is a single one-time purchase that opens all 8 regions and every game; it sits behind the parent gate, and Family Sharing is on. There is no subscription and no in-game currency. Android is coming soon.

Teachers, librarians and education reviewers can ask for a limited review code: see Prof. Nano for schools.

Frequently asked questions

What age is Prof. Nano for?

Prof. Nano is made for children aged 7 to 10. Everything is read aloud, so children who are still learning to read can play.

Is Prof. Nano free?

The first region, Lighthouse Point, with 6 games, is free with no time limit. The Full Version is a one-time purchase that opens all 8 regions and 48 games. There is no subscription.

Does Prof. Nano have ads or collect data?

No. There are no ads, no accounts and no data collection, and purchases sit behind a parent gate.

Does Prof. Nano work offline?

Yes. After the download it works without an internet connection.

Is Prof. Nano on Android?

Not yet. Prof. Nano is on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and an Android version is coming soon.

Which languages does Prof. Nano support?

English and Turkish, with voice narration in both.
