Our games

Mobile games by MEYADE GAMES STUDIO, an independent studio in Turkey. Each page explains what the game is, how it plays, who it's for, what it costs and where to get it.

  • Foot Tennis Stars

    Free foot tennis (football tennis) game for iPhone, iPad and Android: offline matches against computer rivals, or online where iPhone and Android players meet.

    About the game

  • Tomb Maze: Horror Escape

    Free first-person horror maze escape game for iPhone and iPad: 20 labyrinths beneath a collapsed pyramid, creatures that see and hear you, works offline.

    About the game

  • Bolt & Breath: Last Dragon

    Free dragon flight game for iPhone and iPad: fly the last dragon, burn the royal fleet, or switch sides and man the ballista. 57 missions, plays offline.

    About the game

  • NEO: Interactive Horror Story

    Free interactive horror story (text-based, choice-driven) for iPhone and iPad: a missing friend, a clock frozen at 17:34, a time loop, 20 endings; the whole story is free.

    About the game

  • PROTOKOL-Ø: Story Thriller

    Story thriller for iPhone and iPad: you're the AI, and nobody told you. Process files at AION Systems (approve, reject, escalate) and watch the record change. No ads; first modules free.

    About the game

  • Prof. Nano: Kids Science Games

    Hands-on science game for children aged 7 to 10 on iPhone and iPad: 48 mini games in 8 regions, read aloud, no ads, works offline, collects no data; first region free.

    About the game
